ABOUT PAYICHARGE

A simple solution to a modern problem.

We make secure phone charging a useful amenity for customers and a commercial opportunity for host venues.

OUR MODEL

Secure. Convenient. Venue-friendly.

PayiCharge proposes a premium 10-bay secure phone charging station that lets customers safely charge their phones while continuing to enjoy a venue, shop or entertainment space.

The venue-managed model is built around a R20 charging token, customer-selected 4-digit PIN and a master override process for exceptional cases.
